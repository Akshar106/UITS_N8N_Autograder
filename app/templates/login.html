Faculty Autograder

A pilot project at Indiana University · maintained by Akshar Patel (akspate@iu.edu) · built with University Information Technology Services.

Faculty Autograder is a grading assistant for instructors who already use Canvas. An instructor uploads the rubric they grade by, and the tool drafts a score and written feedback for each submission against that rubric. Every draft is then reviewed by the instructor, who can edit or replace any score before it counts for anything.

What it does

  1. Reads an instructor's own Canvas courses and assignments, with their permission.
  2. Drafts a per-question score and feedback for each submission, against the rubric the instructor supplied, using a language model hosted at Indiana University.
  3. Shows every draft in a review queue, quoting the student's own words behind each mark, so the instructor can check the reasoning rather than take a number on trust.
  4. Exports the approved grades as a spreadsheet for the instructor to enter in Canvas.

What it deliberately does not do

  • It never writes a grade back to Canvas. Nothing reaches a student without an instructor approving it first.
  • It never reads courses an instructor does not teach; access is limited to their own.
  • It never operates a login form. Accounts and access are handled by the university's central identity service, on the university's own site.

Data handling

Submission text is sent to the university-hosted grading model solely to draft a score and feedback, and is used for nothing else. Access tokens are held server-side and are never exposed to the browser. Each instructor sees only their own courses, rubrics and drafts.
