Faculty Autograder

This is a pilot tool for IU faculty that connects to your Canvas courses, grades student submissions against a rubric you upload using an AI model, and lets you review and edit every grade before anything is entered into the Canvas gradebook. No grade is ever submitted automatically — faculty review is required for every submission.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your IU account (via IU Login)
  2. Connect your Canvas account (read-only access to your own courses)
  3. Upload a rubric and run AI grading on student submissions
  4. Review, edit, and approve grades before entering them in Canvas

Privacy & data

Student submission text is sent to the configured AI grading model solely to generate a suggested grade and feedback; it is not used for any other purpose. Access tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to the browser.
