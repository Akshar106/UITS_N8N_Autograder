THE DATASET

You are given telco_churn.csv, 7,043 rows, one row per customer:

  customerID       string, unique
  tenure           integer, months with the company (0-72)
  MonthlyCharges   float, dollars

  Contract         categorical: Month-to-month | One year | Two year
  PaymentMethod    categorical, 4 levels
  InternetService  categorical: DSL | Fiber optic | No

  Churn            target, Yes/No — 26.5% Yes

Answer in prose where the question asks you to explain, and in Python
(pandas / scikit-learn) where it asks for code. Code is graded on whether it
would work and whether it is methodologically sound, not on style.

Q6. Spot the leakage (10 points)

This snippet was submitted by a classmate. It reports 94% accuracy.

    scaler = StandardScaler()
    X_scaled = scaler.fit_transform(X)
    X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(X_scaled, y)
    model.fit(X_train, y_train)
    print(model.score(X_test, y_test))

  (a) 6 pts — Identifies that the scaler is fitted on the whole of X before
      the split, so the test rows' means and variances leak into training.
  (b) 4 pts — The fix: split first, fit the scaler on X_train only, and apply
      the fitted scaler to X_test — ideally inside a Pipeline so it cannot be
      got wrong.
